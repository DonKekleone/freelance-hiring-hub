Hire a social media manager around a clear operating system.

Posting is only one part of social management. Buyers should define channels, content responsibilities, approvals, community work and reporting.

Define the actual responsibilities

Content planning.
Copywriting.
Graphic or video production.
Scheduling.
Community replies.
Analytics.
Reporting.
Campaign coordination.

Separate strategy from production

A strategist, creator and community manager may be different people. Do not assume one freelancer is equally strong at every part.

Clarify account access

Use appropriate permissions and keep critical account ownership under business control.

A posting calendar is not automatically a social strategy.Define the audience, objectives and content system before measuring activity.

Agree on approval rhythm

Specify which content requires approval and how quickly the buyer must respond to avoid blocking the schedule.
