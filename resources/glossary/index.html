Freelance Hiring Reference

Freelance hiring terms without the jargon fog.

A practical glossary of the words buyers encounter when sourcing, hiring, paying and managing freelancers.

Scope of work

A written description of what the freelancer is expected to deliver, including boundaries and exclusions.

Milestone

A project stage tied to a meaningful deliverable or review point.

Revision

A change to existing work intended to bring it closer to the agreed brief. Major changes in direction may instead be new scope.

Retainer

An ongoing payment arrangement used to reserve recurring freelance capacity or services.

Fixed-price project

An engagement where a defined scope is completed for an agreed project price.

Hourly project

An engagement where the freelancer bills based on time worked.

Source files

Editable or underlying working files used to create the final deliverable.

Deliverable

A specific item, asset or outcome the freelancer is required to provide.

Scope creep

Expansion of project requirements without corresponding changes to budget, schedule or agreement.

Handoff

The final transfer of completed files, access, documentation and other materials needed to use or maintain the work.

Good freelance projects use ordinary words precisely.Most project conflict is not caused by vocabulary. It is caused by two people using the same words to mean different things.
