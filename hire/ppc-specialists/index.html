Hire Freelancers

Hire a PPC specialist around economics, tracking and campaign control.

Paid advertising requires more than launching campaigns. Buyers should define budget, conversion tracking, creative responsibilities and reporting before hiring.

Define the advertising scope

Search ads.
Social ads.
Shopping campaigns.
Retargeting.
Landing-page input.
Tracking.
Creative coordination.
Reporting.

Separate ad spend from management cost

The freelancer's fee and the advertising budget are different costs. Make both visible before evaluating the engagement.

Keep account ownership

Advertising accounts, pixels, analytics properties and billing relationships should remain under clear business ownership.

Campaign performance is inseparable from measurement.If conversion tracking is weak, optimization becomes guesswork.

Ask how testing works

Understand how the freelancer changes bids, audiences, keywords, creative or landing pages and how long tests are allowed to run.
