Freelancer Costs

Platform fees are part of the real hiring cost.

Marketplace fees, payment charges and service costs can affect the final economics for both buyer and freelancer.

Look beyond the headline price

A quoted freelancer rate may not equal the buyer's final payment or the freelancer's final receipt.

Fees can appear in several places

Buyer service fees.
Freelancer marketplace fees.
Payment processing charges.
Currency conversion.
Withdrawal fees.
Premium hiring services.
Optional promotion or subscription costs.

Fees influence behavior

When marketplace costs are significant, buyers and freelancers may compare the convenience and protections of the platform against direct hiring alternatives.

Verify current pricing directly with the platform.Fee structures change. Use this Hub to understand what to look for, then confirm current numbers before making a decision.

Compare total value

The cheapest payment route is not automatically the best hiring route. Consider discovery, workflow, dispute systems and administrative convenience alongside fees.
