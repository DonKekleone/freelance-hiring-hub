Buyer Template

A freelancer vetting checklist for before you commit.

Use this checklist to verify the person, the work and the project structure before increasing financial or operational exposure.

Identity and professional consistency

Name and professional profiles are reasonably consistent.
Contact information matches the person or business you expect.
Relevant work history can be explained.
Major contradictions have been clarified.

Portfolio

Portfolio work is relevant to your project.
The freelancer can explain their role in the work.
At least some claims can be independently verified where appropriate.
Quality is reasonably consistent across examples.

Project fit

The freelancer understands the deliverables.
Timeline expectations are realistic.
Revision expectations are clear.
Required files and handoff are defined.

Payment structure

Payment stages correspond to meaningful work.
Large upfront exposure has a clear justification.
Payment destination is verified.
Platform or escrow terms are understood.
The checklist does not make the decision for you.Its purpose is to expose missing information before the missing information becomes expensive.
