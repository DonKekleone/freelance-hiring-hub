Freelancer Rates

What does a freelance graphic designer cost?

Graphic design pricing varies with scope, complexity, experience, revision load and the type of deliverables required.

Not all design assignments are comparable

A single social graphic, packaging system and complete brand identity require very different levels of work.

Major cost drivers

Number of deliverables.
Complexity of the design system.
Research and concept development.
Number of concepts required.
Revision rounds.
Source-file requirements.
Turnaround time.

Hourly versus project pricing

Small ongoing work may fit hourly billing, while clearly defined projects can often be priced as a package.

Compare what is included before comparing the number.One quote may include concepts, revisions and source files while another does not.

Define final deliverables

Specify file formats, dimensions, editable files and usage needs before accepting a quote.
