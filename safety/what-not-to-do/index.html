What not to do when hiring a freelancer.

Many expensive freelance mistakes are predictable. Avoiding a few bad habits can improve the project before it starts.

Do not hire from the headline alone

A polished profile, impressive title or strong pitch is not enough evidence by itself. Review relevant work and ask specific questions.

Do not leave scope in your head

If the deliverables, revisions and handoff only exist as assumptions, expect disagreement later.

Do not expose the full budget unnecessarily

Large projects should usually create evidence before the buyer takes maximum financial risk.

Do not give uncontrolled account access

Use appropriate permissions and keep ownership of critical business accounts clear.

Do not change the target casually

Buyers create project failure too. Repeatedly changing direction while expecting the original budget and deadline to remain fixed is a scope problem.

Good hiring is not about distrusting freelancers.It is about designing a project where expectations, ownership and incentives stay understandable for both sides.

Do not skip the handoff conversation

Files, credentials, documentation and ownership should be discussed before the end of the project.
