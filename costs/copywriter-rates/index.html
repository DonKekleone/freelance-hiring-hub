Freelancer Rates

What does a freelance copywriter cost?

Copywriting rates depend on the commercial importance of the work, research requirements, specialization and project scope.

A word count is not the whole project

A short landing-page headline can require more strategic work than a much longer low-stakes article.

Major cost drivers

Sales importance of the asset.
Research requirements.
Industry specialization.
Number of pages or messages.
Interview requirements.
Revision rounds.
Strategy included with writing.

Common pricing structures

Copywriters may charge hourly, per project, per page, per email sequence or through monthly retainers.

Buy the business problem being solved, not merely the number of words.Commercial writing is often valuable because of decisions and positioning, not typing volume.

Clarify scope

Determine who provides research, positioning, offers, design context and final approvals.
