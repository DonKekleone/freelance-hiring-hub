Hire Freelancers

Hire a bookkeeper around the records and workflow you need maintained.

Bookkeeping work may include transaction categorization, reconciliations, reporting and coordination with accounting professionals. Define systems and responsibilities clearly.

Define the bookkeeping scope

Transaction categorization.
Bank reconciliation.
Credit-card reconciliation.
Accounts payable support.
Accounts receivable support.
Monthly reporting.
Record cleanup.
Accounting-system maintenance.

Clarify professional boundaries

Bookkeeping, accounting, tax preparation and financial advice are not identical services. Define what the freelancer is and is not being hired to provide.

Control financial access

Use appropriate permissions and avoid granting broader banking or payment authority than the work requires.

Financial workflows deserve explicit access controls.Convenience should not erase separation between recordkeeping and authority over funds.

Set the reporting rhythm

Agree on deadlines, reconciliation frequency, reporting and how unresolved transactions are escalated.
