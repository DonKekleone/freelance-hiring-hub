Platform Comparison

Upwork vs Toptal: open marketplace versus curated talent.

The central difference is sourcing model: broad candidate access versus a more screened network.

Upwork provides breadth

A large marketplace can give buyers many options across price points and specialties.

Toptal emphasizes curation

A screened network can reduce some of the buyer's initial sourcing burden.

Screening and fit are not the same

Even highly qualified talent must still match the specific project, communication style and technical context.
