Freelance Sourcing Guide

LinkedIn can be a sourcing channel when you know who you are looking for.

Professional profiles, work history and network connections can help buyers discover independent specialists outside traditional freelance marketplaces.

Search by specialty, not just 'freelancer'

Look for the actual professional role, technology, industry or creative specialty required by the project.

Professional history provides context

Profiles can help buyers understand prior roles, industry exposure and connections, but claims should still be evaluated against relevant work.

Direct outreach should be specific

Explain why you contacted the person.
Describe the project briefly.
State the relevant specialty.
Give a realistic timeline.
Avoid generic mass outreach.
Provide a clear next step.
Direct sourcing works better when the outreach proves you selected the person intentionally.A specific message is easier to evaluate than a vague invitation to 'discuss an opportunity.'

Build your own hiring process

When sourcing directly, the buyer is responsible for vetting, scope, payment structure and project administration.
