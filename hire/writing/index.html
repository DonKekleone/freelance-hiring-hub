Choose the writing specialty

Hire for the purpose of the writing, not just the ability to write.

Writing roles overlap, but the commercial objective, audience, research burden and expected deliverable can require very different strengths.

Hire copywriters when the writing needs to drive action

Copywriters typically work on landing pages, sales pages, emails, advertisements and other material designed to influence a decision. The buyer should define the offer, audience, channel and desired action before evaluating samples.

How to Hire Copywriters → Freelance Copywriter Rates → Project Brief Template →

Hire content writers for educational and informational work

Content writers are often a better fit when the goal is to explain, educate, support search visibility or build an ongoing publishing library rather than produce direct-response sales copy.

How to Hire Content Writers → How to Hire Blog Writers → How to Hire SEO Specialists →

Hire blog writers when ongoing publishing is the operating model

Blog writing usually depends on a repeatable editorial process: topics, briefs, research, voice, review and publication. Buyers should decide whether the writer is only producing drafts or also contributing to topic development and content planning.

How to Hire Blog Writers → How to Write a Project Brief → How to Give Better Feedback →

Hire technical writers when subject accuracy matters as much as prose

Technical writers may create documentation, product explanations, procedures or specialist content. Strong writing samples are useful, but buyers should also verify whether the writer can understand and explain the underlying subject accurately.

How to Hire Technical Writers → How to Interview Freelancers → Freelancer Interview Questions →

Hire ghostwriters when authorship and voice are part of the assignment

Ghostwriting requires agreement on voice, research, collaboration, confidentiality, credit and ownership. Long-form work also benefits from milestones so the buyer can review direction before the entire project is written.

How to Hire Ghostwriters → How to Structure Milestones → Intellectual Property Protection →

Hire proofreaders and editors for the level of intervention you need

Proofreading, copyediting and deeper editing solve different problems. Define whether the freelancer is correcting errors, improving style, reorganizing material or substantially rewriting before comparing quotes.

How to Hire Proofreaders & Editors → Scope of Work Template → Revision Policy Template →

Evaluate writing samples for relevance, not polish alone

A strong sample in the wrong format may tell you less than a modest sample that closely matches the audience, subject and objective of your project. Review evidence against the assignment you are actually buying.

How to Review a Portfolio → Portfolio Review Checklist → How to Spot Fake Portfolios → How to Verify a Freelancer →

Define voice, scope and revision expectations before drafting begins

Writing projects become expensive when the target keeps moving. Give the freelancer enough information about audience, objective, references, exclusions and revision boundaries to make the assignment stable.

How to Write a Project Brief → Project Brief Template → Revision Policy Template → Too Many Revisions →

Budget after defining what the writing is expected to accomplish

A short sales page, a technical manual and an ongoing article program should not be priced as interchangeable word counts. Compare rates only after the scope and responsibility are clear.
