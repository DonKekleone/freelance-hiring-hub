Hire Freelancers

Hire a graphic designer for the work you actually need.

Graphic design can mean a single marketing asset or an entire visual system. Define the deliverables before comparing portfolios or prices.

Start with the deliverables

A graphic designer may create advertising assets, social graphics, print materials, presentations, packaging or broader visual systems. The hiring criteria should follow the assignment.

What to define before hiring

Number and type of deliverables.
Required dimensions and formats.
Existing brand guidelines.
Examples or visual references.
Revision expectations.
Source-file requirements.
Deadline.

Review relevant work

Look for examples that resemble the medium and business problem involved in your own project.

Do not hire a portfolio in the abstract.Hire evidence that matches the kind of design work you need.

Clarify the handoff

Agree on exported files, editable files, fonts, licensed assets and any formats needed for future production.
