Hire Freelancers

Hire a podcast editor around the finished listening experience.

Podcast editing can include cleanup, pacing, mixing, show assembly and publishing support. Define what happens between the raw recording and final episode.

Define the editing scope

Noise cleanup.
Mistake removal.
Pacing.
Level balancing.
Music.
Advertisements.
Intro and outro assembly.
Exporting.

Decide how aggressive editing should be

Some shows want natural conversation preserved while others prefer tighter edits. Give the editor examples.

Clarify publishing tasks

Editing and uploading, show notes, chapter markers or distribution are separate responsibilities unless included explicitly.

The cleanest edit is not always the best edit.The goal is a listening experience appropriate to the show.

Establish a repeatable workflow

For ongoing podcasts, consistent file naming, deadlines, feedback and delivery standards save substantial time.
