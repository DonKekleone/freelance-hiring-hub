Freelance Project Problems

The freelancer wants more money mid-project. Ask what changed.

An additional payment request can reflect legitimate new scope, an honest estimating mistake or an attempt to renegotiate after commitment.

Return to the original agreement

Compare the current work with the original deliverables, assumptions and exclusions.

A higher price may be reasonable when

The buyer added deliverables.
Requirements changed materially.
Previously unknown technical conditions appeared.
The buyer requested additional revision rounds.
Timing was accelerated after kickoff.

A higher price deserves scrutiny when

Nothing material changed and the only explanation is that the freelancer underestimated their own work.

Scope change and price change belong together.If the price moves, identify the corresponding change in work, risk or timing.

Document the revised agreement

If both sides agree to continue, update the scope and remaining payment structure in writing.
