Freelance Hiring Template

A revision policy that stops feedback from becoming infinite scope.

Define what a revision is, how many rounds are included and what happens when the buyer changes direction.

Number of included revision rounds.

How long the buyer has to review.

What happens after approval.

Define a revision

A revision should normally refine work toward the agreed brief. A new concept or changed objective may be new scope.

Consolidate feedback

Multiple stakeholders should combine comments into one clear review whenever practical.

Unlimited revisions usually mean undefined completion.A project needs a point where the agreed deliverable becomes accepted work.

Make changes visible

If the buyer changes the underlying direction, update the scope and timeline instead of hiding the change inside another revision round.
