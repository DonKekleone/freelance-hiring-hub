Freelancer Rates

What does a freelance SEO specialist cost?

SEO pricing depends on whether you need an audit, technical work, ongoing strategy, content support or a combination of services.

Define the SEO assignment

SEO can mean keyword research, technical audits, content planning, link work, reporting or implementation.

Major cost drivers

Size and complexity of the website.
Technical implementation required.
Competitive environment.
Content volume.
Reporting requirements.
Local versus national or international scope.
Ongoing versus one-time work.

Project, hourly and retainer models

Audits may fit project pricing while ongoing SEO commonly uses monthly retainers or hourly support.

Ask what work is actually being performed.SEO retainers can be difficult to evaluate if activities and deliverables are not visible.

Avoid guaranteed ranking claims

Search outcomes depend on many variables. Evaluate process, evidence and judgment rather than promises of guaranteed positions.
