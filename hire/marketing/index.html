Choose the marketing specialty

Hire for the channel, system and outcome you actually need.

Marketing roles overlap, but search, paid media, email, social, affiliates and content all require different operating knowledge, tools and success measures.

Hire SEO specialists for search visibility and organic acquisition

SEO work can include technical audits, keyword research, content strategy, on-page optimization and authority building. Buyers should define the search problem and avoid treating guaranteed rankings as a credible project outcome.

How to Hire SEO Specialists → Freelance SEO Rates → How to Hire Content Marketers →

Hire PPC specialists when paid acquisition is the operating channel

Paid media work depends on budget, conversion tracking, creative, targeting and account structure. Buyers should keep advertising accounts, pixels, analytics properties and billing relationships under clear business control.

How to Hire PPC Specialists → Protect Business Accounts & Assets → Project Brief Template →

Hire email marketers for campaigns, automation and lifecycle messaging

Email marketing can include newsletters, promotional campaigns, automations and lifecycle flows. Clarify who owns the list, who manages the platform and which metrics matter before work begins.

How to Hire Email Marketers → How to Hire Copywriters → How to Write a Project Brief →

Hire social media managers for channel operations and community work

Social management may include content planning, publishing, community response, reporting and coordination with designers or video editors. Define which channels and responsibilities actually belong to the role.

How to Hire Social Media Managers → How to Hire Content Writers → How to Hire Short-Form Video Editors →

Hire affiliate marketers when partner distribution is the growth model

Affiliate programs depend on tracking, partner recruitment, attribution, commission structure and clear rules for credit. Buyers should understand how referrals are measured before paying for growth.

How to Hire Affiliate Marketers → Scope of Work Template → How to Negotiate Freelancer Rates →

Hire content marketers when strategy and distribution matter together

Content marketing is broader than writing individual articles. The role may include topic strategy, editorial planning, distribution, search, measurement and coordination with writers or designers.

How to Hire Content Marketers → How to Hire Content Writers → How to Hire SEO Specialists →

Define measurement before evaluating performance

Marketing projects become difficult to judge when success is left vague. Agree on the relevant metrics, reporting cadence and data access before treating performance as a freelancer problem.

How to Write a Project Brief → Scope of Work Template → How to Manage Remote Freelancers →

Keep marketing infrastructure under business control

Advertising accounts, analytics, email platforms, domains, social profiles and audience data should not become inaccessible when the engagement ends.

Source File & Account Protection → Project Handoff Checklist → Intellectual Property Protection →

Budget after defining channel, scope and responsibility

An SEO audit, ongoing social management, paid media operation and email automation build are different engagements. Compare prices only after candidates are pricing the same work.
