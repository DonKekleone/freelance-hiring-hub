Hiring through Toptal: understand what the screening model buys you.

Toptal positions itself around screened professional talent. Buyers should compare the value of curation against project needs, cost and alternative sourcing methods.

Screening changes the sourcing process

A curated network can reduce some of the initial search burden compared with an open marketplace.

Screening does not define project fit

A strong general professional profile does not automatically mean the freelancer is the best match for a specific technical or business context.

Evaluate the engagement model

Understand how matching, billing and project commitments currently work before starting.

Premium sourcing should still be project-specific.The value of screening depends on whether the resulting talent matches the actual work.

Compare total cost and convenience

Buyers should weigh sourcing speed and screening against budget, flexibility and the availability of alternatives.
