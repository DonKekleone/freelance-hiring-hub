Compare what changes the outcome

A useful comparison needs more than one variable.

Fees matter, but buyers can make bad decisions by comparing only fees. Hiring options also differ in talent access, control, speed, coordination, payment structure, communication and project risk.

A simple comparison framework

Talent: who can you realistically find through this option?
Control: how much freedom do you have over process and communication?
Cost: what are the direct and indirect costs?
Speed: how quickly can you source and begin?
Risk: what happens when the project goes wrong?
Administration: what does the buyer have to manage personally?

Compare the operating model

An agency, freelancer, marketplace and direct-hire relationship can all work. The important question is which operating model fits the size, complexity and management burden of the specific project.
