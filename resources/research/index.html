Freelance Research

Research the freelance market without turning every statistic into a headline.

This section organizes reports, datasets and research that can help buyers understand independent work, hiring patterns, rates, platforms and project risk.

Primary sources first

When possible, use original surveys, government data, research institutions and first-party reports rather than articles that merely repeat someone else's numbers.

Separate evidence from interpretation

A report can establish what respondents said or what a dataset measured. Broader conclusions should be clearly distinguished from the underlying evidence.

Research should make the Hub harder to fool.The point is not to collect impressive numbers. It is to improve the quality of hiring decisions.

Build a dated archive

Keeping older research alongside newer releases can make long-term changes easier to see without pretending every old number is still current.
