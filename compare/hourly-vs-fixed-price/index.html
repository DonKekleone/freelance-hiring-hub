Hiring Model Comparison

Hourly vs fixed price: choose based on uncertainty.

The best pricing model depends less on preference and more on how clearly the work can be defined before it begins.

Hourly works well when the path is uncertain

Research, debugging, maintenance and evolving work can be difficult to price accurately in advance.

Fixed price works well when boundaries are visible

A clear deliverable with stable requirements is easier to quote as a project.

Hourly risks

Poor visibility into time spent.
Open-ended scope.
Buyer anxiety about efficiency.
Difficulty predicting final spend.

Fixed-price risks

Scope disagreements.
Hidden assumptions.
Change requests treated as included work.
Quality sacrificed to protect margin.
The pricing model cannot rescue unclear scope.A badly defined project can fail under either hourly or fixed-price billing.

Hybrid approaches

Some projects use fixed milestones for defined work and hourly billing for unknowns or ongoing support.
