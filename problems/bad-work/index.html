Freelance Project Problems

Bad freelance work needs diagnosis before it needs drama.

Determine whether the failure is skill, scope, communication or expectations before deciding whether to revise, replace or stop the project.

Define what is actually bad

A useful assessment is specific. Identify inaccurate work, technical defects, poor execution, missing requirements or a mismatch with agreed examples.

Compare delivery against evidence used to hire

If the delivered quality is dramatically weaker than relevant portfolio work, ask questions about who produced those examples and who performed the current work.

Decide whether the problem is repairable

Are the core skills adequate?
Does the freelancer understand the criticism?
Can the work be corrected within reasonable revisions?
Has trust in the work itself been damaged?
Would another freelancer need to start over?
Do not keep revising a capability problem forever.Revisions fix execution. They do not manufacture skills that were never there.

Preserve usable work

Before ending the engagement, secure files, credentials and any completed assets that another freelancer may be able to continue.
