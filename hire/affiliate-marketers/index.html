Hire Freelancers

Hire an affiliate marketer around the economics and partner system.

Affiliate marketing can involve partner recruitment, program operations, tracking, commissions and promotion. Define the actual system before hiring.

Define the role

Affiliate recruitment.
Partner outreach.
Program setup.
Tracking.
Commission structure.
Creative support.
Reporting.
Partner management.

Clarify what the freelancer controls

Some affiliate marketers recruit partners while others manage software, reporting or promotion. The title alone does not define the job.

Make attribution visible

The buyer should understand how referrals are tracked and how disputes over credit are resolved.

Affiliate marketing is an incentive system.If tracking and economics are unclear, more partners can create more confusion instead of more revenue.

Protect the brand

Set rules around claims, traffic sources, bidding, spam and other promotional behavior.
