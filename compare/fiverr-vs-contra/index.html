Platform Comparison

Fiverr vs Contra: packaged services versus independent portfolios.

The two platforms encourage buyers to discover freelance work in different ways.

Fiverr starts with the offer

Buyers often browse predefined services and packages.

Contra starts more with the professional

Independent profiles and portfolio presentation play a larger role in discovery.

Defined work favors easy comparison

A packaged service can be convenient when scope is standardized.

Custom work needs more discussion

Projects with uncertain scope benefit from deeper conversations before commitment.

Is the deliverable standardized?
Do you need interviews first?
How much customization is required?
Are source files important?
How much revision work is expected?
The more custom the project, the less useful a package title becomes.Read the actual scope and talk through anything that matters.
