Hire Freelancers

Hire a copywriter for the commercial problem, not the word count.

Copywriters can work on landing pages, emails, ads, sales pages and positioning. Define what the writing needs to accomplish before comparing samples.

Start with the asset and objective

Writing an email sequence, homepage and paid advertisement requires different constraints and decision-making.

Give the writer useful inputs

Audience.
Offer.
Product or service details.
Existing positioning.
Research or customer data.
Desired action.
Brand voice.
Approval process.

Evaluate relevant copy

Look for work serving similar commercial objectives rather than simply judging whether the prose sounds impressive.

Good copy is built from information.A writer cannot recover customer insight, offer clarity and positioning that the buyer never supplies.

Clarify revisions

Define who approves the copy and whether strategy or major repositioning is included in the assignment.
