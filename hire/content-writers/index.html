Hire Freelancers

Hire a content writer around audience, subject and distribution.

Content writing works better when the buyer defines who the piece is for, what it should accomplish and how much subject expertise is required.

Define the content type

Articles.
Guides.
Case studies.
Product education.
Thought leadership.
Newsletters.
Website content.

Match expertise to subject difficulty

General writing ability and subject-matter understanding are different things. Technical or regulated topics may require deeper expertise.

Set research expectations

Define whether the writer receives source material or is expected to conduct interviews, research and fact checking.

Good content is not merely grammatically correct.It should be useful to the intended reader and appropriate for the channel where it will appear.

Clarify editorial workflow

Agree on outlines, drafts, revisions, approvals and publishing responsibilities.
