Hiring Model Comparison

Marketplace vs direct hire: convenience versus control.

Marketplaces can simplify discovery and administration. Direct hiring can give buyers more control over the relationship and workflow.

Marketplaces bundle infrastructure

Discovery, profiles, messaging, payment systems and dispute processes can reduce the amount of setup required from the buyer.

Direct hiring increases responsibility

Without a marketplace, the buyer may need to handle sourcing, contracts, payments, verification and project administration independently.
