Freelance Project Problems

Scope creep is what happens when little additions stop being little.

Projects drift when new requests are added without adjusting the budget, timeline or definition of completion.

Recognize scope creep early

One small extra request may be harmless. Repeated additions can quietly change the economics of the entire project.

Common examples

Additional pages or deliverables.
Extra formats or sizes.
New integrations.
Additional revision rounds.
New stakeholders and approval steps.
Work originally assigned to someone else.

Compare every addition with the agreed scope

Ask whether the request is necessary to satisfy the original deliverable or whether it creates a new deliverable.

Every new requirement consumes something.If scope increases, time, price or another requirement usually needs to move with it.

Use change control without bureaucracy

A simple written message stating the new work, added cost and timeline impact is often enough.
