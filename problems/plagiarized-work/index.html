Freelance Project Problems

If the delivered work is plagiarized, treat it as a project integrity problem.

Copied writing, stolen designs or reused assets can create legal, reputational and operational problems beyond simple poor quality.

Verify before accusing

Similarity alone is not always plagiarism. Gather the suspected original source and compare the work carefully.

Preserve evidence

Save the delivered files.
Record the source of the suspected original.
Keep relevant messages and scope documents.
Document what originality or licensing was promised.
Avoid altering the evidence before the dispute is understood.

Stop using questionable work

If ownership is uncertain, avoid publishing or distributing the material until the issue is resolved.

Ask for an explanation

Give the freelancer an opportunity to explain licensed assets, references, collaboration or other circumstances.

Portfolio theft and delivery plagiarism are related warning signs.Both are reasons to strengthen verification before future engagements.

Review available remedies

Depending on the agreement and hiring channel, options may include replacement work, refund discussions, platform disputes or professional legal advice for serious cases.
