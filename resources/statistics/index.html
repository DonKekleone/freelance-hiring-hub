Freelance Research

Freelance hiring statistics need context, dates and sources.

This section is designed for sourced data about freelance work, hiring behavior, platforms and independent talent. Statistics should be treated as dated evidence rather than timeless facts.

What belongs here

Freelance workforce estimates.
Buyer and business adoption data.
Platform usage data.
Freelancer earnings research.
Remote-work data.
Skills-demand research.
Independent-work trends.

Always keep the date attached

Freelance markets change. A statistic from several years ago can still be useful historically, but it should not be presented as a description of the current market.

Population matters

A survey of one platform's users is not automatically representative of all freelancers, all businesses or the entire labor market.

A statistic without a source, date and population is decoration.The research section should make those three things easy to see.

Research library coming together

As the Hub adds sourced datasets and reports, this page can become the index for quantitative freelance-hiring evidence.
