Freelance Project Problems

The freelance project is over budget. Find out what changed before adding more money.

Budget overruns usually come from underestimated work, expanding scope, repeated revisions or unknowns that became visible after kickoff.

Reconstruct the original estimate

Compare what was originally priced with what has actually been requested and delivered.

Look for the source of the overrun

Additional deliverables were added.
Requirements changed.
More revisions were requested than planned.
Technical unknowns appeared.
The original estimate was unrealistic.
Buyer delays created rework.
Freelancer inefficiency increased time.

Separate sunk cost from remaining value

Money already spent should not automatically justify spending more. Evaluate the remaining work on its own economics.

Do not approve 'just a little more' without a new finish line.Any additional budget should purchase a clearly defined remaining outcome.

Reset the project if continuing

Create an updated scope, remaining budget and delivery plan rather than continuing under assumptions that already failed.
