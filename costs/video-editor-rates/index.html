Freelancer Rates

What does a freelance video editor cost?

Video editing prices depend heavily on format, footage quality, finished length, graphics, revision load and ongoing volume. Compare the production requirement before comparing quotes.

Finished minutes do not tell the whole story

A 30-second advertisement can require more editing than a much longer talking-head video. Complexity, source footage and creative expectations matter more than duration alone.

Major cost drivers

Amount of raw footage.
Finished video length.
Short-form versus long-form workflow.
Motion graphics and animation.
Captioning and graphic treatment.
Sound cleanup or design.
Number of revision rounds.
Recurring monthly volume.

Hourly pricing versus per-video pricing

Hourly work can make sense when the scope is fluid. Per-video or package pricing can make recurring production easier to budget when requirements are consistent.

Compare the workflow, not only the rate.An editor with a repeatable process can often produce more useful output than a cheaper editor who requires constant correction.

Ask what the quote actually includes

Clarify music licensing, captions, graphics, thumbnails, project files, revisions and turnaround time before comparing prices.
