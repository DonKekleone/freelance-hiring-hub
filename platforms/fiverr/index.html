Hiring freelancers on Fiverr: understand the package before buying.

Fiverr makes it easy to browse packaged freelance services, but buyers still need to compare scope, revisions and deliverables carefully.

Packaged services simplify browsing

Buyers can often start with a predefined offer rather than writing a full job post.

Packages are not standardized

Two similarly named services can contain very different deliverables, revision limits and turnaround expectations.

Review relevant examples

A seller's marketplace history is useful context, but portfolio relevance still matters.

Read the boundaries

What is included?
What costs extra?
How many revisions are included?
What files are delivered?
What is the stated turnaround?
Are commercial-use or source-file rights included where relevant?
Convenient purchasing does not remove the need for scope.The faster the buying flow, the more important it is to understand exactly what the package contains.

Verify current terms

Check Fiverr directly for current fees, protections and marketplace policies.
